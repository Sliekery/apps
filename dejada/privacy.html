Dejada privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Dejada is a score keeper for padel, tennis and pickleball on iPhone and Apple Watch, made by Tom Graulus.

The short version

We don't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your matches, names and settings stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Apple Watch and Apple Health

On Apple Watch, a match runs as a workout so the app stays on screen. With your permission it's saved to Apple Health as a workout (time and active energy). The app doesn't read any Health data. You can turn saving off in the Watch app, and change access in the Health app. Health data is never sent to us, never shared and never used for advertising.

Names

Team and player names you type are stored only on your devices, with your matches. Matches played on Apple Watch are passed to your iPhone directly, between your own devices.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Your data, your control

Delete a match by swiping it in History, or delete everything by removing the app. Workouts can be deleted in the Health app.
