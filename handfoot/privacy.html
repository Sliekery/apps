Privacy policy: Hand & Foot Score Sheet

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The short version

We don't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your games, team names and settings stay on your iPhone.

What's stored on your iPhone

The game in progress and its undo steps, finished games, saved team names, house rules and settings. They're kept in the app's own storage and are part of your iPhone backup, which Apple handles. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

The one-time unlock (Hand & Foot Pro) is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Sharing

If you share a result, it goes only where you send it, using the iPhone share sheet.

Your data, your control

Delete a finished game by swiping it in History, a saved name in Settings › Saved team names, or everything by deleting the app.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, children included.
