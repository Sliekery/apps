Talewell — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Talewell collects no data.

  • There are no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Recordings, written-out text, photos and names are stored only on your iPhone. They are part of your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or your computer), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.
  • Recordings are written out by Apple's speech recognition on the iPhone itself. Talewell asks for on-device recognition only, so the audio isn't sent anywhere for this.
  • The microphone is used only while you record a story. Photos you add are chosen with the system photo picker; Talewell can't see the rest of your library.
  • Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you share it yourself: a story, the book, or a copy of everything.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • If you email support (gentlepace.app@gmail.com), we only use your message to answer you.
