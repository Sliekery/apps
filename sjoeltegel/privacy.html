Sjoeltegel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Sjoeltegel collects no data.

  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your scores, game history and runs are stored only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • If you email support (gentlepace.app@gmail.com), we only use your message to answer you.
