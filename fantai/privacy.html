FanTai (番台記數) privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The short version

We don't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your tables, scores and friends' names stay on your device.

What the app stores

The app stores the names you type, the rules you pick and the hands you record. They're saved in the app's own storage on your device and are never sent anywhere. The developer can't see them.

Sharing

When you share a settle-up, the app makes an image or text and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes; the app uploads nothing itself.

Lock Screen

If you've unlocked Pro, the balances of the table in play appear in a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. This is shown by iOS on your device only. You can turn Live Activities off in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Your data, your control

  • Delete a table by swiping it in History.
  • Remove a friend in Settings → Friends.
  • Delete everything by removing the app.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Email gentlepace.app@gmail.com or use the support link on the app's App Store page.
